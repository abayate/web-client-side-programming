NEON DEFENSE

A tower-defense game · HTML + CSS + JavaScript · by Anthony Bayate

Defend the Core

Enemies march along the neon path toward your core. Spend credits to build towers beside the path and stop them before they get through.

  • Pulse – fast, cheap, reliable
  • Cannon – slow shots with area damage
  • Frost – slows enemies in range
  • Sniper – huge range and damage

Click a tower to upgrade or sell it. Survive all 20 waves to win!
