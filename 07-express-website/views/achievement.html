Legacy of Fear

Although infamous for his cruelty, Voldemort achieved remarkable influence through manipulation, magical skill, and fear.

Notable Achievements

  • Mastered a vast range of dark spells
  • Created a terror-driven political movement
  • Defied death through the Horcrux method
  • Inspired generations of fear across the magical world

Ambition may elevate, but fear never sustains.
