History of Voldemort

Explore the dark history of Lord Voldemort, from his early years to his rise to power.

1926 - Birth

Tom Marvolo Riddle is born on December 31, 1926. His mother dies shortly after his birth.

1938 - Hogwarts Begins

Tom Riddle begins his studies at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He becomes a model student and is soon made prefect.

1943 - The Chamber of Secrets

Tom Riddle discovers the Chamber of Secrets and opens it, releasing the Basilisk. Multiple students die before the chamber is sealed.

1945 - Graduation

Tom Riddle graduates from Hogwarts. Despite the Chamber incident, no evidence links him to it. He disappears after graduation.

1960s - Rise to Power

Lord Voldemort begins recruiting followers known as Death Eaters. He establishes himself as a powerful dark wizard and begins to gather supporters.

1970-1981 - The First Wizarding War

Voldemort launches his first bid for power, terrorizing the wizarding world. The Order of the Phoenix, led by Albus Dumbledore, opposes him. The war ends with Voldemort's apparent downfall.

1995 - The Second Rise

Voldemort returns to power with the help of his followers. The Second Wizarding War begins.

1998 - The Final Battle

The final confrontation takes place at Hogwarts. Voldemort is defeated, and the Second Wizarding War ends. The wizarding world is forever changed.

Key Relationships

Albus Dumbledore: Voldemort's greatest rival and the only wizard he feared.

Death Eaters: His loyal followers who spread terror and helped him carry out his plans.

Harry Potter: The wizard who would ultimately be his downfall.
