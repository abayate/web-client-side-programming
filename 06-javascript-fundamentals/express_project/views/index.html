Introduction

Discover the life and times of Lord Voldemort, the Dark Lord. A complex figure in wizarding history, Voldemort's quest for immortality and power shaped the magical world for generations.

The Dark Lord

Born as Tom Marvolo Riddle, he rose to become the most feared wizard of all time. His influence spread across the wizarding world, creating division and fear among witches and wizards.
