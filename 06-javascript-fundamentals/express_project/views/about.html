About Voldemort

Learn about the origins and rise of Tom Marvolo Riddle, better known as Lord Voldemort.

Early Life

Tom Marvolo Riddle was born on December 31, 1926, to a Muggle father and a pure-blood witch mother. His mother died shortly after his birth, and he was raised in an orphanage in London, never knowing his true heritage until he received his Hogwarts letter.

At Hogwarts

As a student, Tom Riddle was exceptional - a model student, charming and intelligent. He excelled in his studies, made prefect, and earned the trust of his teachers. However, his true nature was hidden beneath this façade of perfection.

The Quest for Immortality

Obsessed with death and driven by a fear of mortality, Tom Riddle began to study the darkest aspects of magic. His research led him to create Horcruxes - objects imbued with fragments of his soul - as a means to achieve immortality and escape death itself.

The Dark Lord

Taking the name "Lord Voldemort" - an anagram of his birth name - he emerged as a powerful dark wizard. He gathered followers known as Death Eaters and built a network of supporters in the wizarding world, spreading fear and promoting his ideology of pure-blood supremacy.

Philosophy

  • Belief in pure-blood superiority
  • Rejection of love and human connection
  • Pursuit of power above all else
  • Fear of death and obsession with immortality
  • Belief that he alone could rule the wizarding world
